FAQ

Questions before you start

Do you offer a complete digital solution?

Yes — website, mobile application, SEO / GEO / AEO, social media marketing / PPC, WhatsApp lead generation with Business automation, and AI automation. One partner for the full stack.

Where should my business start?

Most businesses start with a high-converting website, then a mobile app, layer SEO / GEO / AEO, add social & PPC for demand, close on WhatsApp with automated follow-ups, then scale with AI automation. We’ll recommend the best order on your free call.

What is SEO / GEO / AEO?

SEO ranks your website on Google. AEO helps you win answer engines and featured snippets. GEO helps AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity cite and recommend your brand.

What is Social Media Marketing / PPC?

Organic social builds trust with content and community. PPC (Meta, TikTok, Google Ads) puts budget behind the same offer for fast leads, traffic, or sales — we run both with shared creative and reporting.

Do you set up WhatsApp Business and lead automation?

Yes — WhatsApp Business profile and API setup, click-to-WhatsApp from ads and your site, instant replies, lead tagging, CRM or sheet sync, and automated follow-up sequences for bookings and sales.

What is AI automation?

We connect AI agents and workflow automations to your website, CRM, forms, and tools — so routing, summaries, and back-office tasks run without manual busywork.
