FAQ

Questions before you start

Do you offer a complete digital solution?

Yes. We start with your website, then mobile apps & ASO, social media marketing & ads, branding, and grow visibility with SEO, AEO, and GEO — one partner for the full journey.

Where should my business start?

Most businesses start with a high-converting website, then add a mobile app and ASO, social marketing & ads, and layer SEO / AEO / GEO to get found. We’ll recommend the best order on your free call.

What is ASO and how do you rank an app?

ASO (App Store Optimization) helps your app rank in App Store & Google Play search. We optimize titles, keywords, screenshots, descriptions, ratings & reviews so more people find and download your app organically.

What is SEO vs AEO vs GEO?

SEO ranks your website on Google. AEO helps you win answer engines and featured answers. GEO helps AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity cite and recommend your brand.

What is social media marketing vs social ads?

Social media marketing is organic content — posts, stories, community — that builds trust. Social ads are paid campaigns that target the right audience fast for leads, traffic, or sales.
