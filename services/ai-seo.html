AI SEO

AI SEO writes the pages, facts, and answers so a brand can be cited when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, or Claude.

Start AI SEO See US and UK projects

How AI SEO is developed

01

Question research

Collect the exact questions buyers ask in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Claude, plus the Google queries already in the report.

02

Entity facts

Lock one business name, what it sells, where it serves, and the proof an answer engine can repeat without guessing.

03

Answer pages

Build a page for each priority question: a direct answer first, then the detail, the offer, and the source.

04

Schema and structure

Add FAQ, product, or local schema and clear headings so the answer can be extracted.

05

Citations

Place the same facts on pages and profiles that answer engines already read, so the brand is not only on its own site.

06

Engine checks

Ask the same query in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Claude. Record whether the brand is named, and what is missing.

07

Revise

Rewrite the weak answers, add the missing proof, and check the four engines again after the next release.
