WhatsApp Leads

WhatsApp Business and lead automation. An ad click or a website visit becomes a chat, then a booked call, an order, or a sale.

Start WhatsApp leads See the setup

How WhatsApp leads are set up

01

Business profile

The WhatsApp Business profile: name, photo, description, hours, and the number customers will message.

02

Click-to-WhatsApp

Buttons on the website, ads, and Google profile that open a chat with a ready-made first message.

03

Instant replies and menus

A first reply, a short menu, and the questions that sort a new chat into booking, order, or support.

04

Human handoff

The chat moves to a person when the lead is qualified, with the answers already collected.

05

CRM or sheet

Each new chat is written to the sheet or CRM the business already uses, with name, need, and source.

06

Follow-up

A short sequence for people who asked and did not book, so the lead is not left in the chat.

07

API when volume needs it

The WhatsApp API is added when the business needs more than the Business app: shared inbox, templates, and more than one person on the same number.

What the customer can do

The same path on the site, on an ad, and in the chat.

From the website

A WhatsApp button on the page the visitor is already reading, with the offer named in the first message.

From an ad

Click-to-WhatsApp on Meta so the ad opens a chat instead of a form the person may not finish.

From the team

A person picks up a qualified chat, and the follow-up still runs if that chat goes quiet.
