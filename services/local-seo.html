Local SEO

A subcategory of SEO / AEO / GEO. Local SEO is for map results, “near me” searches, and city pages that turn a nearby search into a visit or booking.

Start local SEO See developed projects

How local SEO is developed

01

Locations and categories

List every address or service area, the primary category, and the cities the business must be found in.

02

Google Business Profile

One profile per location: name, address, phone, hours, services, photos, and a matching website link.

03

City and service pages

A page for each city or neighborhood, with the offer, proof, and a clear way to call or book.

04

Name, address, phone

Use the same business details on the site and on the profiles that mention the business.

05

Reviews

Ask recent customers for reviews and reply to them, so the map listing stays active.

06

Local citations

Add the business to relevant free directories with the same details used on Google.

07

Map tracking

Check map rank and calls for the priority “near me” and city queries, then fix the pages that are missing.
