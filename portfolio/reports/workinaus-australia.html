1. Campaign Architecture & Lead Qualification

Recruiting working holiday makers and skilled trades to Australia requires overcoming trust barriers and geographical distance. By deploying native creator video testimonials showing real UK and European backpackers who landed jobs in Sydney, Melbourne, and Byron Bay, cost per lead dropped from $3.50 to $1.90 AUD.

Dynamic Pre-Qualification

Meta Instant Forms ask for passport country, current visa status, and trade skills before submission, eliminating unqualified entries before they hit the CRM.

Creator Whitelisting

Partnered with popular Australian travel creators on Instagram Reels and TikTok, running dark-post ads with authentic travel storytelling.

Leads are automatically greeted within 90 seconds via WhatsApp Business API, boosting verified CV submission rates to 64%.
