1. The ASO Challenge & Strategic Approach

In the fiercely competitive UK food delivery market across London, Manchester, Birmingham, and Edinburgh, Deliveroo needed to capture top placement for transactional food searches and rapid grocery delivery queries:

  • Hyper-Local UK Food Terms: Optimized metadata for British culinary queries ("takeaway near me", "late night food delivery London", "sunday roast delivery", "grocery delivery under 30 mins").
  • Localized Creative Gallery: Redesigned iOS screenshots highlighting beloved British restaurant partnerships (Dishoom, Wagamama, Five Guys) and live rider GPS map tracking.
  • Seasonal A/B Creative Testing: Deployed dynamic seasonal screenshot sets for major football tournaments, bank holidays, and festive delivery rushes, boosting conversion by +36.4%.
