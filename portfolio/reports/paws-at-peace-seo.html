1. What the site offers

Paws at Peace provides at-home pet euthanasia, hospice and palliative care, quality-of-life consultations, private and communal cremation, and pet-loss grief support. The public site says the team serves the five New York City boroughs, Delaware County, Pennsylvania, and selected communities in Chester and Montgomery counties, including parts of the Main Line. Hours on the site are Monday–Friday 8am–8pm and Saturday–Sunday 8am–6pm. Contact on the site is info@pawsatpeace.com and (917) 694-2890.
