6-Month Organic Search Growth Campaign · Client: Apex Hotels Group · Domain: apexhotels.co.uk

🇬🇧 United Kingdom London · Edinburgh · Bath · Glasgow Hospitality & Spa Resorts +311.6% Organic Traffic £51,912/mo OTA Fees Saved

Performance Data: Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Ahrefs & Core Web Vitals Audits (Baseline vs Month 6)

Executive Summary: Transforming Organic Search into the Primary Direct-Booking Channel

Prior to engagement, Apex Hotels was heavily dependent on Online Travel Agencies (OTAs like Booking.com and Expedia), forfeiting 18% commission fees on every reservation. Despite 10 prestigious boutique hotel locations across London, Edinburgh, and Bath, the website suffered from severe Core Web Vitals failures (4.8s mobile LCP), unindexed landing pages, and virtually zero top-3 rankings for non-branded commercial terms. Over 6 months, MubeCodes overhauled technical performance, deployed localized Schema architecture, launched destination content hubs, and secured Tier-1 UK hospitality backlinks.

❌ Before SEO (Month 0 Baseline)

  • 34,500 Monthly Organic Clicks: Restricted mostly to exact brand name searches.
  • Page 3-7 Trapped Keywords: "Boutique hotels Edinburgh" stuck at #42; "Hotels near Tower Bridge" stuck at #38.
  • Heavy OTA Dependency: ~74% of online reservations routed through high-fee aggregators.
  • Critical Technical Bottlenecks: 142 broken 404 URLs, 67 redirect chains, no structured hotel schema.
  • Failing Mobile Web Vitals: 4.8s Largest Contentful Paint causing mobile booker drop-off.

✅ After SEO (Month 6 Post-Optimization)

  • 142,000 Monthly Organic Clicks (+311.6%): Over 107,500 additional monthly organic visitors.
  • 428 Keywords in Top 3 (#1-#3 SERP): Ranked #1 for Bath spa breaks, #2 for Edinburgh boutique hotels.
  • £51,912/Month Saved in OTA Fees: Direct reservations jumped from £86.4K to £374.8K per month.
  • 100% Technical Health: 0 crawl errors, valid Hotel/Room/Offer JSON-LD schemas across all 10 locations.
  • Flawless 94/100 Core Web Vitals: Sub-1.4s load times nationwide across 4G and 5G connections.

3. The Exact 6-Month Execution Roadmap

The systematic methodology deployed to generate these results:

Phase 1 · Month 1

Technical Cleanse & Speed

Crawled 2,400 URLs. Eliminated 142 broken 404 links, resolved redirect chains, and compressed 1.2GB of unoptimized room photography to achieve green Core Web Vitals.

Phase 2 · Month 2

Schema & Local Alignment

Implemented JSON-LD structured data for Hotel, Room, Offer, and FAQPage. Cleaned Google Business Profiles across London, Edinburgh, and Bath properties.

Phase 3 · Month 3

Destination Content Hubs

Published comprehensive local travel itineraries ("48 Hours in Bath", "Top Things to Do Near Tower Bridge") capturing thousands of upper-funnel holiday seekers.

Phase 4 · Month 4

High-DA Hospitality PR

Secured high-authority contextual backlinks on The Caterer (DA 68), VisitScotland (DA 85), and The Times UK (DA 94), boosting domain trust.

Phase 5 · Month 5

Booking Engine CRO

Streamlined direct reservation UX, added "Best Rate Guarantee" badges on organic landing pages, and optimized checkout funnel speed for mobile users.

Phase 6 · Month 6

Page 1 Domination

Stabilized Top 3 rankings across competitive target terms, resulting in 142,000 monthly clicks and £51,912 in monthly OTA middleman fee savings.
