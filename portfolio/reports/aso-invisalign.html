1. The ASO Challenge & Strategic Approach

Invisalign required an App Store Optimization strategy to target patients researching teeth straightening, dental scans, and cosmetic orthodontic consultations. We optimized the metadata funnel to convert casual oral healthcare searchers into active registered app users:

  • High-Intent Dental Keywords: Embedded top dental conversion queries ("clear aligners", "invisible braces", "teeth straightening", "dentist near me") across title, subtitle, and hidden keyword fields.
  • Visual CRO & Video Preview: Designed a 5-step panoramic gallery displaying virtual smile simulations (SmileView™) and remote doctor photo check-ins, lifting install conversion rate by +32.1%.
  • Ratings & Review Optimization: Automated in-app prompt triggers post-first scan, raising the active average rating to 4.8★ and securing top placement in iOS Medical & Health categories.
