1. The On-Demand ASO Playbook

SFYKEA pioneered waterless, doorstep automotive cleaning in Pakistan's major metropolitan centers. Converting searchers into first-time booking users required addressing local consumer skepticism around timing and pricing through transparent store assets.

  • Hyper-Localized Geo Keywords: Targeted localized keyword variants ("car wash at home lahore", "doorstep car cleaning karachi", "auto detailing islamabad") to capture intent right at the point of need.
  • Step-by-Step Funnel Preview: Replaced generic splash screens with a 3-step action gallery: (1) Set live GPS pin, (2) Choose waterless detailing package, (3) Track service rider arrival live on map.
  • Play Store Listing Localization: Implemented bilingual keyword tags (English + Urdu script search terms) to maximize search visibility across diverse user demographics.
