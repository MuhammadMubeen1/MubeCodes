1. The ASO Challenge & Strategic Approach

MoodWise needed to differentiate itself from overcrowded meditation and subscription-heavy mindfulness apps by emphasizing minimalism, CBT-backed daily tracking, and strict on-device data privacy:

  • Privacy-First & CBT Keyword Targeting: Optimized title and subtitle metadata around high-intent self-care queries ("minimalist mood tracker", "cbt journal", "anxiety daily log", "private emotion diary").
  • Calming Visual Creative Redesign: Replaced busy screenshot layouts with soothing dark-mode pastel visuals emphasizing "No account required" and "100% on-device encrypted logs".
  • Tap-Through-Rate (TTR) Optimization: Conducted 4-way icon and subtitle split testing on App Store Connect, driving an immediate +28.4% increase in organic download conversion.
