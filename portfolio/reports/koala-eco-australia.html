1. Creative Testing & Advantage+ Architecture

Australian consumers are deeply conscious of environmental sustainability and natural ingredients. By shifting creative away from sterile product studio shots to warm, sunlit Australian kitchen counters showcasing native lemon myrtle, peppermint, and eucalyptus extracts, engagement surged by 210%.

Advantage+ Shopping Scaling

Consolidated 12 fragmented ad sets into Meta's Advantage+ algorithm, providing maximum budget flexibility and decreasing blended CPA from $28.50 to $16.20 AUD.

Starter Bundle Focus

Pivoted ad hooks to the "Best Sellers Trio Kit", raising the Average Order Value (AOV) from $38 to $74 AUD while keeping customer acquisition cost flat.

Subscription Lifetime Value

Implemented post-purchase refill replenishment flows via email and SMS, driving 58% of new customers into recurring bi-monthly subscribers.
