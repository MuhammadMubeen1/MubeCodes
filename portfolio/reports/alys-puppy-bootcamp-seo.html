4. SEO work for this site

  • Separate landing pages for boot camp, board-and-train, online courses, trained dogs, and each leash, so one homepage is not asked to rank for every offer.
  • Location language on the in-person page, using the service area the business actually trains in, plus a page for owners who train online from other states.
  • Dog pages that keep breed, weight, collection, and availability in the title and the first paragraph.
  • Product schema on the four Good Walker leashes and the membership tiers, using the prices already published.
  • Blog answers that stay tied to a course or a product, instead of sitting apart from the offer.
  • AI answers for the same training queries are written up in the AI SEO report.
