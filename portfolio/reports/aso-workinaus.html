1. The Employment & Visa ASO Strategy

Australia attracts hundreds of thousands of international working holidaymakers, skilled migrants, and local job seekers annually. Competing against monolithic job boards required positioning WorkinAUS as the fast-track application tool built for mobile-first applicants.

  • High-Intent Search Keywords: Targeted "working holiday jobs australia", "farm work visa 417", "hospitality jobs sydney", and "mining jobs western australia".
  • 1-Tap Apply Screenshot Showcase: Replaced boring job list mockups with dynamic previews showing instant resume parsing, employer chat, and instant interview scheduling. Elevated store page conversion by +36%.
  • Cross-Platform Parity: Synchronized metadata across the Apple App Store and Google Play Store for consistent brand recognition across backpacker and expat communities.
