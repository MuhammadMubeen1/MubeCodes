1. The ASO Challenge & Strategic Approach

DICE needed to establish dominance over legacy ticketing platforms by emphasizing fair, transparent ticketing, anti-scalping technology, and curated live music discovery across London, Bristol, Manchester, and Glasgow:

  • Concert & Gig Discovery Terms: Optimized titles, subtitles, and backend keywords targeting music lovers searching for "concert tickets uk", "gig guide london", "festival lineup", and "club night tickets".
  • Interactive Creative Showcase: Crafted bold, typography-forward App Store screenshots displaying personalized artist recommendations, Spotify playlist syncing, and instant QR code entry.
  • Conversion Rate Optimization: Emphasized "No hidden fees" and "Safe ticket transfers" in the first two gallery frames, propelling the download conversion rate by +29.2%.
